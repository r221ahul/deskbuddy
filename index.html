How to use Buddy

Touch

  • Tap: happy face and a heart.
  • Double tap: a wink.
  • Five fast taps: Buddy gets annoyed.
  • Hold for a second: sleep. Tap to wake.
  • Shake the phone: Buddy gets dizzy.

Voice

  • Tap the mic, speak at once, wait for the dots to turn into the mouth.
  • Ask follow-ups, Buddy remembers the last few messages.
  • No mic? Use Type.

Buttons

  • Quiz me: one NABARD-style MCQ on a random topic. Say your answer aloud.
  • News: three headlines on RBI, agriculture credit and the economy.
  • Focus 25: a 25 minute study timer. Buddy stays calm, then chimes. Tap again to stop.

Ideas to try

  • "Explain PMFBY in 30 words."
  • "Difference between repo and reverse repo."
  • "What did RBI announce today?"
  • "Quiz me on rural development."
  • "Give me 3 ESI facts about climate finance."
  • "Plan my next two hours of study."

Setup: open from an https link, allow the microphone, add the Gemini key in settings, then use the browser menu and Add to Home screen. The screen stays on while the app is open. After 11 pm it dims on its own.
